# Attlas Equity

Attlas Equity is a privately held, founder-owned venture studio based in Dublin, Ireland, operating across Ireland and Africa. We build the digital infrastructure that connects emerging markets to the global economy. 
Through strategic partnerships, proprietary technology development, and a deep understanding of underserved regions, we create platforms that empower entrepreneurs, developers, and communities to thrive in a rapidly changing world. 
Since our founding, we have focused on building—not buying—companies, with a commitment to long-term value creation and genuine impact. Learn more about our vision below.
We believe every region deserves access to world-class digital infrastructure. More than just a venture studio, Attlas Equity is a launchpad for meaningful technology. 
We help founders, developers, and partners develop the systems they need to thrive: agility, deep technical insight, collaborative ecosystems, and uncompromising integrity.

## Open Source & Development at Attlas

Attlas Equity is committed to strategic development and transparent execution. Many of our internal tools, frameworks, and components are being built with long-term sustainability in mind. 
Please keep in mind that we are a lean, founder-led operation, so we may not be able to respond in detail to every external inquiry. We appreciate your patience and understanding. 
The Attlas-core repository is a monorepo containing the main components of our internal infrastructure, and is a good place to start for collaborators and partners.
Ready to build with us? Check out our general contributing guidelines as well as any repository-specific documentation. Thank you!
